Introduction

Lion dancing has become one of my favorite hobbies here at UF because it allows be to express my culture, perform to hundreds of people, choreograph pieces, stay physically fit, and meet new people.

Tips

  1. Practice practice practice.
  2. Create a strong base with your legs and core.
  3. Emphasize sharp movements and playful expressions.
  4. Listen carefully and follow the beat of the drum.
  5. Communicate with your lion dance partner.
